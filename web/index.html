        How it works

        Movie Night compares rating patterns from MovieLens viewers. High ratings support related titles; low ratings reduce that support. Each suggestion names the liked movie contributing the most to it.

        The recommendation catalog uses ratings through 2018. You can add newer or missing titles manually to your watched list. Ratings and watched entries stay in this browser; export the list if you want a backup.
